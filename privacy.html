Parade of Monsters

Privacy policy · last updated 28 October 2026

Parade of Monsters does not collect, sell or share your personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your progress lives only on your phone or in your browser: brains, mutations, skins, badges, records, the sound setting and the look you pick for Parade Royale. Deleting the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, deletes it. It is never sent to us.

Playing online (Parade Royale)

Online matches connect players' devices directly to each other. To set up a connection, the game uses a matchmaking service (PeerJS), Google's and Metered's address-lookup (STUN) servers and, when two devices cannot reach each other directly, Metered's relay. These services see your device's IP address while you play, as any internet connection does. We do not receive or keep it.

The name you type for a match is shown to the other players in that match, and to no one else. It is not stored anywhere except on your own device.

Playing against bots needs no connection.

Fonts

The game loads its two fonts from Google Fonts when you are online, so Google sees your IP address when the fonts load.

Ads

The iPhone app shows no ads. On game websites such as CrazyGames, the site's own ad and privacy terms apply.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no personal information from anyone.
